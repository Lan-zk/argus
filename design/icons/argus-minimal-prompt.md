+Use case: style-transfer

Constraints: no typography, no actual text, no letters, no watermark, no decorative marks, no sparkles, no red, no vivid colors, no realistic eyeball, no large pupil, no glossy plastic, no glass, no thick extrusion, no dramatic shadow, no additional objects, no multiple alternatives, no presentation board.
